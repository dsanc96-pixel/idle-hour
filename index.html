The Idle Hour: Support

When do new puzzles arrive?

A new issue of six puzzles appears every day at midnight, your local time.

Can I play days I missed?

Yes. Open "Back issues" to play any of the last 30 days. Back issues count toward your totals but not your streak.

How do I change the difficulty?

Use the Easy, Medium and Hard switch above the puzzle cards on the front page.

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes it.
