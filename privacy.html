The Idle Hour: Privacy Policy

The Idle Hour does not collect, store or share any personal information.

Your puzzle progress, streak and settings are saved only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. When you are online it may download its display fonts from Google Fonts and new puzzle content (words, clues, questions and facts) from this website, which is hosted by GitHub Pages. No information about you is sent with those requests beyond what any web request includes, such as your IP address. Fun facts may show a "Read more" link; tapping it opens the source in your browser.

If you choose "Share my results" or "Print this issue", the text or page is handed to the iOS share sheet or printer you pick. What happens next is up to the app or printer you chose.

Questions: see the support page.
